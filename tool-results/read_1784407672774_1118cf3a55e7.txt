    13→Adaptation Notes — Restaurant Template → Pressure Washing (internal
    14→only)
    15→
    16→This section is for your eyes only — remove before handing to lavadoatp
    17→or any other client. These are the domain-specific decisions made
    18→converting the restaurant v3 template to a pressure-washing /
    19→power-washing service business, and why.
    20→
    21→+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
    22→| ADAPTATION 1 — Menu → Services catalog                                |
    23→|                                                                       |
    24→| Pressure washing has no single "menu." Replaced with a service        |
    25→| catalog (Residential / Commercial / Specialty categories) supporting  |
    26→| three pricing models — fixed, starting-at, and quote-only — because   |
    27→| most jobs in this trade are priced by surface area and condition, not |
    28→| a flat menu price.                                                    |
    29→|                                                                       |
    30→| ADAPTATION 2 — Reservations → Bookings & Crew Scheduling              |
    31→|                                                                       |
    32→| There is no seating or floor plan in this business. The               |
    33→| floor-plan/table-assignment UI was replaced with a calendar-grid job  |
    34→| scheduler plus a crew-assignment field, since the operational unit is |
    35→| a scheduled on-site job, not a seated table.                          |
    36→|                                                                       |
    37→| ADAPTATION 3 — Before/After Slider promoted to a first-class          |
    38→| component                                                             |
    39→|                                                                       |
    40→| Before/after transformation is this industry's single                 |
    41→| highest-converting asset — arguably more than for restaurants, where  |
    42→| a photo gallery is closer to atmosphere-setting than proof-of-work. A |
    43→| dedicated drag-slider component, paired and sourced from the same     |
    44→| job's real Instagram content, now gets its own Step 3 subsection      |
    45→| instead of being folded into a generic gallery.                       |
    46→|                                                                       |
    47→| ADAPTATION 4 — Video is treated as mandatory, not optional            |
    48→|                                                                       |
    49→| Pressure washing sells through motion — the "oddly satisfying"        |
    50→| transformation clip is the format that actually drives engagement in  |
    51→| this niche. The Instagram scrape pipeline (Step 0A) was extended to   |
    52→| pull Reels/video, not just static images, and a zero-video result is  |
    53→| logged as a launch-blocking data gap, not a cosmetic one.             |
    54→|                                                                       |
    55→| ADAPTATION 5 — Zero-AI-media rule extended to video, and to           |
    56→| before/after authenticity specifically                                |
    57→|                                                                       |
    58→| Stock or AI-generated imagery undermines trust on any local-service   |
    59→| site, but a faked or digitally "enhanced" before/after pair is close  |
    60→| to consumer-fraud territory in this specific trade — operators have   |
    61→| been called out publicly for staged before/afters. This prompt treats |
    62→| real, same-job, unedited pairs as a hard requirement, not a style     |
    63→| preference, and removes the restaurant template's Unsplash            |
    64→| stock-photo fallback entirely (see Step 3.1).                         |
    65→|                                                                       |
    66→| ADAPTATION 6 — Location → Service Area                                |
    67→|                                                                       |
    68→| Many pressure-washing operators run mobile crews across a metro       |
    69→| radius rather than a single storefront. The schema and the location   |
    70→| section now support a zone/radius coverage model in addition to (or   |
    71→| instead of) a fixed address.                                          |
    72→|                                                                       |
    73→| ADAPTATION 7 — Schema.org type changed                                |
    74→|                                                                       |
    75→| Restaurant → HomeAndConstructionBusiness, with an areaServed property |
    76→| — the accurate, validator-recognized type for this trade.             |
    77→|                                                                       |
    78→| ADAPTATION 8 — Quote Calculator moved into Phase 1                    |
    79→|                                                                       |
    80→| An instant ballpark estimate (service type + property size → price    |
    81→| range) is close to an industry-standard conversion tool for           |
    82→| pressure-washing sites, unlike restaurants, where there's no real     |
    83→| equivalent. Built now, not flagged as a Phase 2 upsell.               |
    84→|                                                                       |
    85→| WHATSAPP WIDGET — deliberately left unchanged                         |
    86→|                                                                       |
    87→| The v3 spec (Step 3.2) already followed the correct pattern: a real   |
    88→| wa.me click-to-chat button, not a custom fake chat UI pretending to   |
    89→| be WhatsApp. Nothing about pricing or scheduling logic changes that.  |
    90→| Only the pre-filled greeting text was reworded for a quote-request    |
    91→| context — mechanically, it is the same widget.                        |

  1155→    what was scraped vs. manually supplied.

  1158→    shared passwords. Owner sets their own password via Supabase email
  1159→    invite.
  1160→
  1161→-   Supabase free tier: 500MB DB + 2GB storage + 50,000 MAU — covers a
  1162→    single business indefinitely at demo and early-live scale. Video
  1163→    storage is the constraint to watch, not rows — budget accordingly
  1164→    once Reels are in play.

  1167→    portal working, pitch recurring maintenance plans and crew dispatch
  1168→    as a monthly SaaS retainer — the database is already structured for
  1169→    it.

  1172→    → Site Editor at any time to pull new photos and video without a
  1173→    redeploy.

  1175→-   Bolivia-specific: currency defaults to BOB. Default service area:
  1176→    Santa Cruz de la Sierra metro + configurable radius, editable in
